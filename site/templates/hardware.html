Hardware

The test machine is a 15-inch 2-in-1 laptop (Lenovo IdeaPad 5 2-in-1 15IPH11) with an Intel Core Ultra 5 322, the entry Core Ultra 5 in the Panther Lake (Core Ultra Series 3) family. This page condenses the sourced hardware reference (last researched $ref_date).

How to read the labels: Vendor Intel, Lenovo or Microsoft said it. Independent a third party measured it. Derived arithmetic or inference from cited figures. Unverified no confirming source found. Local observed on the test unit.

The laptop

From the Lenovo PSREF spec sheet Vendor: CPU options Core Ultra 5 322, 325 or Core Ultra 7 355; 2 x DDR5-5600 SO-DIMM, dual channel, up to 32 GB; 2 x M.2 PCIe 4.0 x4; 15.3" WUXGA IPS or 2.5K OLED touch display; 60 or 84 Wh battery with a 65 W USB-C adapter; sold as a Copilot+ PC.

Why this matters for the results

  • Memory bandwidth bounds LLM decode. DDR5-5600 is below the SoC's DDR5-6400 ceiling, and the NPU, iGPU and CPU share it. That is why the three devices decode Phi-3-mini within a small factor of each other. Derived
  • A small CPU and a small GPU. With 6 threads and 2 Xe3 cores, this is the lowest Panther Lake configuration. Bigger SKUs will show smaller CPU penalties and a much stronger iGPU.
  • Peak TOPS are vendor ceilings. 46 NPU + 18 GPU TOPS are INT8 peaks under ideal conditions (QRG footnote 1). This site reports measured latency and throughput instead.

Independent reviews

  • Notebookcheck: Core Ultra 5 322 first review Independent (14-inch sibling): Cinebench R23 multi 7862, single 1782; weak iGPU, over 15 h Wi-Fi browsing; no NPU testing.
